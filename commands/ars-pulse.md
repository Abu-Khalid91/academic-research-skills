---
description: ARS research pulse — on-demand research-trends and business-insight digest
---

Launch the Research Pulse skill to produce a dated, cited digest of research trends and their business/monetization angle. Runs live web searches across four buckets — frontier AI/LLM & agentic-research trends, the user's active research domain, academic-research-tooling trends, and business/monetization trends — and synthesizes findings with a "so what" note per finding.

**Broad scan:**
```
/ars-pulse what's trending in AI research right now
```

**Scoped to a domain:**
```
/ars-pulse citation hallucination detection — what's new, any business angle?
```

**Deep dive on a prior finding:**
```
/ars-pulse deep-dive on [paper/trend name] — how does it compare to our approach?
```

Skill location: `research-pulse/` directory.
